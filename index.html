Before you book

Small things that save the most trouble.

Book trains early

IRCTC opens 60 days ahead. Popular routes fill within days.

Check road status

Hill roads in Himachal and Ladakh can shut with snow or rain. Look before you leave.

Carry cash and ID

Remote places have patchy UPI and ATMs. Keep a valid photo ID for permits and hotels.
